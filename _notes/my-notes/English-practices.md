# 2026.6 CET-6
## The first set

Facing with growing globalization, students realize that a deep understand of traditional Chinese cluture helps cultivate clutural confidence.

### 1

作为一名新时代青年大学生，我们更需要拥有坚定的文化自信，向世界展示中国面貌。
As a new era youth studying in college, we are going to have tough culture confidence, so express Chinese look for world.

(1)<u>As college students in the new era</u>, we should (2)<u>stand firm in our cultural confidence</u> and show the world a (3)<u>vibrant</u>, authentic China.

(1)虽然名词组成的英语词组在英文中很常见，比如college students, study habits etc..，但除常用词组外，还是需要使用其他形式来表示修饰语，比如新时代青年，不建议new era youths，而是youths in new era或youths of new era。

(2)拥有坚定的文化自信，在语气和主动性上不如“坚定站在文化自信的立场上”和“增强文化自信”，firm既可以是名词noun，还可以是形容词adj，亦可以是副词adv，stand in sth，支持...；常用的加强有strengthen和reinforce。

(3)vibrant，充满活力的，鲜艳的。

### 2

一个人的生活和思维习惯深受这片土地上过去人们产生的文化影响，尽管他可能丝毫没有意识到这一点，当他与其他人交谈时，也在向对方展示自己对传统文化的理解与判断，只有对传统文化有深入的理解，他才能正确地向其他人说明传统文化中独特的文化现象从何而来？文化现象根植于自然与人文环境中，它的诞生是合理且自然的，因此，深入理解传统文化的人，才能在其他人不理解本国传统文化时予以包容和理解，在好奇产生原因时予以解释和回答，在不了解别国传统文化时予以尊重和敬意，彰显真正的文化自信。

A man's live and thought habits deeply are effected by last people's living in this land culture.Although he maybe is awareless about it. When he talkiing with other, also show them his comprehending and value judgement for traditional culture. Only have a deep understanding for tradition culture, he can correctly explain where are special culture phenomenon from to others.Culture phenomenon roots in specific culture and nature enviroment, it comes into being naturally and rationally.So, a man really comprehending the traditional culture, giving tolerance and understanding when someone doesn't know his homeland's traditional culture, giving answer and reason when someone is curious, and giving respect and esteem when he doesn't learn about other countrys' culture. This is real culture confidence.

(1)<u>A person's way of life and way of thinking are deeply</u> shaped by (2)<u>the culture of the generations who lived on this land before them</u>, even if the person is (3)<u>unaware</u> of it. (4)<u>When talking with others</u>, people also (5)<u>reveal</u> their understanding of, and judgment about, traditional culture. (6)<u>Only with a deep understanding of their own tradition</u> can they correctly explain where particular cultural phenomenoa come from. At the same time,cultural phenomena are rooted in a specific cultural and natural environments, and they arise naturally and for good reasons. Therefore, a person who truly understands  traditional culture is patient with those who do not know their homeland's traditions, explains the reason when others are curious, and shows respect for other countries' cultures even when unfamiliar with them. This is true cultural confidence.
